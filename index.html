Patient counts for CAR-T and BiTE are drawn from a de-duplicated registry compiled by Ragnar Gunnarsson, MD/PhD (status 1 July 2026): figures are the registry’s n (counted), i.e. overlapping cohorts/publications have been removed so patients are not double-counted. All counts are aggregate and non–patient-identifiable, provided for research and educational purposes only; they are a snapshot that may be incomplete and must be verified against primary sources before any clinical or decision-making use. Publication, trial and preprint data are retrieved live from their providers (U.S. National Library of Medicine / NIH, EMBL-EBI Europe PMC, EMA) and remain © those providers. The chart reflects reported activity (publications + curated counts), not a patient census.
